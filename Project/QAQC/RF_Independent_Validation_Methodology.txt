RF INDEPENDENT VALIDATION METHODOLOGY
============================================

Each of the 150 accepted RF outer-LOOCV models was reconstructed from its original fold-specific training dataset.

Previously selected RF hyperparameters and the stored RF random seed were reused. No tuning was repeated and the validation data had no influence on model configuration.

For each run, one genuinely held-out occurrence and 100 independent pseudo-background cells were predicted.

The held-out probability was recalculated only to verify exact model reconstruction and was compared against the probability stored during the original LOOCV workflow.

Pseudo-background cells are not confirmed mineralization absences. Subsequent confusion matrix metrics therefore quantify discrimination between known occurrences and pseudo-background.
