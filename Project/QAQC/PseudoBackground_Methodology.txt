GUMUSLER MPM - PSEUDO-BACKGROUND METHODOLOGY
==================================================

Script:
Project/Models/Create_PseudoBackground.R

Purpose:
Create a common pseudo-background candidate pool for subsequent RF, XGBoost and SVM modelling.

Fixed methodological decisions:
- Grid resolution: 25 x 25 m
- Known occurrence count: 5
- Exclusion-buffer distance: 50 m
- Buffer geometry: circular Euclidean distance
- Boundary rule: distances <= 50 m are excluded

Interpretation:
The 50 m buffer does not represent the physical thickness of mineralized veins. It is an exclusion zone intended to prevent immediately adjacent and potentially mineralized cells from being labelled as pseudo-background.

Candidate-pool definition:
All grid cells located more than 50 m from every known occurrence and not themselves occurrence cells.

Important class interpretation:
Pseudo-background cells are not confirmed barren or true absence locations.

Sampling decision:
The selection of 100 pseudo-background cells and the 30 repeated samples will be performed later within the common model-validation workflow. RF, XGBoost and SVM will use identical background samples for each repeat and validation fold.

Leakage-control decision:
During leave-one-occurrence-out validation, background sampling, preprocessing and model tuning will be performed inside the training workflow. The held-out occurrence will never enter model fitting.

Candidate-pool size produced: 9536

Predictors:
Pb_OK, Zn_OK, Cu_OK, Lithology, Dist_Fault, Dist_Silicified, Dist_Brecciated
