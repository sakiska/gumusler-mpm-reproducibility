RANDOM FOREST LOOCV EVALUATION METHODOLOGY

Evaluation runs: 150
Repeats: 30
LOOCV folds: 5

Evaluation is based on probabilities assigned to held-out known occurrences.
Each known occurrence was held out once per repeat, producing 30 independent held-out predictions for each occurrence.
Overall, repeat-level, fold-level and occurrence-level probability summaries were calculated.
Occurrence-level 95% confidence intervals use the Student t distribution across the 30 repeats.
Permutation importance was summarized both for encoded model-matrix columns and for the seven original predictors.
Lithology dummy-variable importances were summed within each run before original-predictor importance was summarized.

ROC-AUC, specificity, precision, recall and F1 were not calculated because independently verified absence locations are not available.
OOB error is retained as a training diagnostic and must not be interpreted as independent spatial validation.
Full-grid threshold and occurrence-capture evaluation will be performed after full-grid RF prospectivity prediction.
